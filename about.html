About the team

Why 2.0?

Because we're the second chapter of Glitch — and the name, the spirit, and the mission survived the rebuild.

The origin story

The name survived. The team was rebuilt.

2016 GLITCH 5854 Community-based veteran FRC team, hosted at UNC Asheville. Competed 2016–2020, then paused during the pandemic.
2022 GLITCH 2.0 FRC 8727 · Asheville High School / SILSA. Registered in 2021, rookie season 2022 — and still going.

THE 2.0 MEANS: THE GLITCH NEVER LEFT — IT JUST FOUND A NEW HOME.

It started with Glitch 5854. For four seasons (2016–2020), a community robotics team hosted at the University of North Carolina Asheville competed in FRC under the name Glitch. When the COVID-19 pandemic hit, the team paused.

Then a mentor stepped up. A mentor from Glitch 5854 approached the Asheville High School / SILSA engineering director about reforming the team. In the summer of 2021, a brand-new FRC team was registered for the AHS engineering department — moving from a small room at UNCA into the AHS/SILSA engineering room.

We kept the name. The root name "Glitch" was retained — but with only three students who had ever attended a competition, we officially started as a rookie team in 2022, which is why our team number is 8727 and our name is Glitch 2.0.

Same name, same mission, new generation. That's the whole story behind the 2.0.

The robot archive

Every season, a new machine.

From a rookie-year crawler to a swerve-chassis district champion. Each robot taught us something the next one needed.

SEASON 2026 · REBUILT™
CHASSISBOT
the 2026 competition robot
▣ DISTRICT EVENT WINNER AUTONOMOUS AWARD

Our 2026 champion. A swerve chassis running CTRE TalonFX / Phoenix 6, built around a fixed-angle flywheel shooter, an intake roller, and a two-motor indexer. Strong, repeatable PathPlanner autonomous carried it to a 15–3–0 record and the win at the UNC Asheville district event.

SWERVE DRIVETRAIN FIXED 45° FLYWHEEL SHOOTER PATHPLANNER AUTO 32–17–0 SEASON DISTRICT #11
SEASON 2024 · CRESCENDO
MELODIE
a.k.a. Beluga
▣ 2× BLUE BANNER WORLDS 2024

The first swerve robot. Melodie brought the team its first-ever winners Blue Banners — twice. A 26.5" × 26.5" chassis with four REV MAXSwerve modules and TPU tread, a full-width over-the-bumper intake, and a pivoting shooter that could score up into the speaker and down into the amp from one mechanism. Endgame was a custom hook on a single telescoping climber, and drivers got bright LEDs plus haptic feedback — an ongoing team signature.

SWERVE DRIVETRAIN PIVOTING SHOOTER OVER-THE-BUMPER INTAKE LED + HAPTIC FEEDBACK TELESCOPING CLIMBER
SEASON 2023 · CHARGED UP
MEGASTUF
the goose
▣ EXCELLENCE IN ENGINEERING FIRST SUSTAINABILITY ×2

Our most complex build. MEGASTUF ran a two-degree-of-freedom counterbalanced arm on a 320° turreted base — controlled with a state-space model and a full physics simulation for fluid scoring trajectories. The turret spun on a 12-inch 3D-printed pulley (where the name came from), all on a custom Corner Omni West Coast drivetrain built for agility on a budget.

2DOF ARM 320° TURRET STATE-SPACE CONTROL 3D-PRINTED PULLEY CUSTOM DRIVETRAIN

SEASON 2022 · RAPID REACT
CRONKBOT
rookie year
▣ ROOKIE ALL STAR HIGHEST ROOKIE SEED

Where it all started. The rookie-year robot proved this team was already thinking differently: a belt-driven ball elevator with automatic indexing, print-in-place Mecanum intake wheels, an adjustable shooting hood that could be changed between matches, and a dual-action climber with two static and two kinematic arms that hit a very high traversal success rate.

BELT ELEVATOR + INDEXER ADJUSTABLE HOOD DUAL-ACTION CLIMBER 3D-PRINTED INTAKE

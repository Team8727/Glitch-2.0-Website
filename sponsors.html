Our founding schools

The campus that raised the team.

Glitch 2.0 was born in the engineering department shared by Asheville High School and SILSA — two schools, one building, one student-led robotics team. Here's who they are and how they're part of the build.

SILSA · Asheville City Schools · Grades 9–12

School of Inquiry and Life Sciences at Asheville

The "school within a school" on the Asheville High campus. SILSA is a nationally recognized magnet built on inquiry-based learning and the life sciences — a hands-on, question-driven model that is exactly how robotics works. Team members come from SILSA's student body, and its classrooms sit steps from the engineering room where we build.

419 McDowell Street · Asheville, NC 28803 · (828) 350-2700
Visit SILSA Website
AHS · Asheville City Schools · Comprehensive High School

Asheville High School

Asheville High — "the Cougars" — is Asheville's public high school and home to the engineering and Career & Technical Education programs that host our team. Glitch 2.0 was registered for the AHS engineering department in 2021, and we still design, machine, and assemble out of its engineering room today.

Corporate & foundation sponsors

Our support ladder.

Every tier of support keeps this team running — from a single battery to a full season. Click any logo to visit our partners. Want in? Email us.

Title Sponsor $10,000+ The top of the pit. Title-level support underwrites the entire season — parts, travel, registration, and the full competitive experience for every student.
Platinum Sponsor $5,000+ The backbone of the build. Platinum partners fund the drivetrain, the electronics, and the travel that gets us to every district event.
Gold Sponsor $3,000+ The material that makes the robot real. Gold partners fund the raw stock, the tooling, and the components that become our machines.
Silver Sponsor $1,000+ The fuel in the pit. Silver partners keep the batteries charged, the outreach running, and the everyday essentials covered.

01 About the team

Engineering, welcome, and a little bit of glitch.

We're Glitch 2.0 (FRC 8727) — a welcoming, all-inclusive, student-led robotics team at Asheville High School in Asheville, North Carolina. We build robots, learn real engineering, and work to get young people excited about STEM in our community.

The name is legacy. Our roots go back to Glitch 5854, a community team hosted at UNC Asheville that competed from 2016–2020 before pausing during the pandemic. In the summer of 2021, a mentor from that team approached the AHS/SILSA engineering director about reforming — and Glitch 2.0 was born for the Asheville High engineering department, keeping the original name and the original spirit.

Because only three of us had ever been to a competition, we officially started as a rookie team in 2022 — and won Rookie All Star in our very first event. Since then we've grown into a team that competes for wins, takes home judged awards, and treats outreach and volunteering as seriously as the robot.

We're part of the FIRST North Carolina (FNC) district, we run a full business and sponsorship program, and every member gets hands-on experience — from the drivetrain to the drive team.

02 The robot archive

Every season, a new machine.

From a rookie-year crawler to a swerve-chassis district champion — here's the robot garage. Each one taught us something the next one needed.

SEASON 2026 · REBUILT™
CHASSISBOT
the 2026 competition robot
▣ DISTRICT EVENT WINNER AUTONOMOUS AWARD

Our 2026 champion. A swerve chassis running CTRE TalonFX / Phoenix 6, built around a fixed-angle flywheel shooter, an intake roller, and a two-motor indexer. Strong, repeatable PathPlanner autonomous carried it to a 15–3–0 record and the win at the UNC Asheville district event.

SWERVE DRIVETRAIN FIXED 45° FLYWHEEL SHOOTER PATHPLANNER AUTO 32–17–0 SEASON DISTRICT #11
SEASON 2024 · CRESCENDO
MELODIE
a.k.a. Beluga
▣ 2× BLUE BANNER WORLDS 2024

The first swerve robot. Melodie brought the team its first-ever winners Blue Banners — twice. A 26.5" × 26.5" chassis with four REV MAXSwerve modules and TPU tread, a full-width over-the-bumper intake, and a pivoting shooter that could score up into the speaker and down into the amp from one mechanism. Endgame was a custom hook on a single telescoping climber, and drivers got bright LEDs plus haptic feedback — an ongoing team signature.

SWERVE DRIVETRAIN PIVOTING SHOOTER OVER-THE-BUMPER INTAKE LED + HAPTIC FEEDBACK TELESCOPING CLIMBER
SEASON 2023 · CHARGED UP
MEGASTUF
the goose
▣ EXCELLENCE IN ENGINEERING FIRST SUSTAINABILITY ×2

Our most complex build. MEGASTUF ran a two-degree-of-freedom counterbalanced arm on a 320° turreted base — controlled with a state-space model and a full physics simulation for fluid scoring trajectories. The turret spun on a 12-inch 3D-printed pulley (where the name came from), all on a custom Corner Omni West Coast drivetrain built for agility on a budget.

2DOF ARM 320° TURRET STATE-SPACE CONTROL 3D-PRINTED PULLEY CUSTOM DRIVETRAIN

CRONKBOT
rookie year

Where it all started. The rookie-year robot proved this team was already thinking differently: a belt-driven ball elevator with automatic indexing, print-in-place Mecanum intake wheels, an adjustable shooting hood that could be changed between matches, and a dual-action climber with two static and two kinematic arms that hit a very high traversal success rate.

04 Impact

Robots are the excuse. People are the point.

We measure success in more than match wins. Since our rookie year we've treated outreach, community service, and building good humans as engineering-adjacent priorities — and the judges have noticed.

◎

Community & STEM outreach

We volunteer in our local community and work to get young people excited about STEM — because a robotics team shouldn't just stay in its own building.

◆

Life skills for students

Members build real skills in engineering, project management, public speaking, and teamwork — hands-on, student-led, and owned by the students doing the work.

⬡

Business & sustainability

We run sponsorship and business planning as an active team function. Judged awards are a real priority — not a side activity.

WORLD RECORD

In 2024 we set the world record for most Sustainability Awards in FIRST history, overtaking team 948 — then followed it with a 2026 season that added more. It's a record that says everything about who we are.

06 Get involved

There's a seat at the pit table.

Whether you're a student, a parent, a mentor, or a company — there's a role for you. Glitch 2.0 is built by everyone who shows up.

Students

No experience required — we teach everything. Learn mechanical design, wiring, programming, strategy, or business. Join the build, drive the robot, or run the pit. Your first year is about learning; every year after is about leading.

GRADES 9–12 · ASHEVILLE HIGH SCHOOL / SILSA

Mentors

We're always looking for engineers, teachers, and professionals to guide students. Mentor on mechanical, electrical, software, or business — or just bring your experience and your patience. It's some of the most rewarding volunteer work around.

NO ROBOT EXPERIENCE NEEDED

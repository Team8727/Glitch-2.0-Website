Our origins

We're 2.0 because this is the second chapter of Glitch.

Our story starts with Glitch 5854 — a community team at UNC Asheville that competed from 2016 to 2020. When the pandemic paused it, a mentor from that team helped rebuild it in 2021 for Asheville High School's engineering program. We kept the name, kept the spirit, and started fresh as a rookie in 2022. That's the whole story behind the 2.0.
